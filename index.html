What is—and isn’t—changing

Only this demo repository. The working explore site is untouched. Colors and typography remain close to the existing site so the comparison is about arrangement and emphasis. The material is a September 30 snapshot, not a current calendar.

The reference preserves the current structure and proportions, but uses a small, fixed photo pool and the same review-friendly discovery interaction as the alternatives. For the exact live behavior, open the original homepage.

The earlier five style-only samples are retained in their folders but are no longer the comparison being proposed.
